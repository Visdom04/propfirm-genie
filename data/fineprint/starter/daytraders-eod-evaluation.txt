How to pass
- Reach the profit target.
- Trade at least 2 Qualifying Days : days that meet the minimum daily profit, follow the rules and end within consistency.
- Keep your best day at or below 50% of your total profit. A small tolerance applies.
- Never touch the drawdown level or exceed the Daily Loss Limit.
After you pass
Stop trading: dropping below the profit target can void the pass. Activate your EOD Pro Account within 10 days for $5 , or the pass is forfeited.
Good to know
- No time limit and no resets.
- Evaluations are unlimited.
