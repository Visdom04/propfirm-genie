Activation Fee For Your Elite Sim Funded Account
This article will explain how the Activation fee works in Elite accounts
Updated over 4 weeks ago
Table of contents
At Top One Futures, once you successfully complete your Evaluation and qualify for a sim funded account, there is a one-time activation fee of $149 . This fee applies to all account sizes and is required before you begin trading your sim funded account.
Good to know: the $149 activation fee is the same for every Elite account size and is paid only once, when you transition from the Evaluation to your Elite Sim Funded account.
How Does the Activation Fee Work?
- Once you pass your Evaluation, you will receive a notification .
- You will be prompted to pay the $149 activation fee before receiving your sim funded account login credentials.
- After payment and review of your account, your sim funded account will be activated, and you can begin trading.
What To Do After You've Paid the Activation Fee
Once you've paid the activation fee, your Elite Sim Funded account will appear in your dashboard and trading platform. However, before you can begin trading, there are a few important steps you must complete:
Step
What to do
1. Complete KYC verification
All traders are required to successfully complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification before trading on any Simulated Funded Accounts with Top One Futures.
2. Sign your trader contract
After KYC is approved, you will be prompted to review and sign your trader contract.
3. Start trading
Once your contract is signed, your account is fully activated and you're ready to begin your trading journey with Top One Futures.
To learn exactly where to complete your verification and sign your contract, please see How to Complete Your KYC Verification .
Final Thoughts
The $149 activation fee is a simple, one-time step to transition from an evaluation trader to a sim funded trader. It ensures a smooth onboarding process, allowing you to start trading your sim funded account and work toward your financial goals with Top One Futures.
