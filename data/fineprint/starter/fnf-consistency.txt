Is there any consistency rule to be followed in the FundedNext Futures Challenge & FundedNext Account?

The Consistency Rule in the Challenge Phase (Legacy & Flex)
During the Challenge Phase , Legacy and Flex apply a 40% Consistency Rule : your profit on any single day cannot exceed 40% of your total profit target . If it does, the total profit required to pass is recalculated .
Formula: 40% × Profit Target = Daily Profit Threshold
Recalculation: Required Total Profit = Highest Daily Profit ÷ 40%
Example — $50,000 Legacy Challenge (Profit Target: $3,000):
- Daily threshold: 40% × $3,000 = $1,200 .
- If you earn $1,500 in a day: $1,500 ÷ 0.40 = $3,750 . You'd now need $3,750 total to pass instead of $3,000.
Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily have no consistency rule during the Challenge Phase — you can hit the target in a single day or across multiple days.
The Consistency Rule on the FundedNext Account (Rapid Pro)
Once funded, the 40% Consistency Rule applies only to the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account . Here it works as a withdrawal gate : if your biggest single-day profit is more than 40% of your total profit, you must keep trading until your results are more consistent.
There's no fixed number of days — it depends on your performance — but it takes at least 3 days to satisfy. Once your highest profit day is 40% or less of total profit, you're eligible to withdraw.
Formula: Highest Profit Contribution = (Highest Profit Day ÷ Total Profit) × 100%
- 40% or less → consistency met (withdrawal eligible).
- More than 40% → not met (keep trading).

The Consistency Rule on the FundedNext Account (Direct Account)
The FundedNext Futures Direct Account uses a 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule as a withdrawal gate: your highest single-day profit ever recorded on the account must stay at 20% or less of your current cycle's total profit . If your best day is more than 20% of the cycle total, you must keep trading until the cycle grows enough to bring it back within range.
What makes it perpetual is that your best day carries forward across every cycle — once set, it becomes the figure every future cycle is measured against, until you record an even bigger day.
Formula: Highest Profit Contribution = (Highest Profit Day Ever ÷ Current Cycle Total) × 100%
- 20% or less → consistency met.
- More than 20% → not met (keep trading).
Because 20% is one-fifth, your cycle total simply needs to reach at least 5× your best day ever to stay consistent.

Which accounts have no consistency rule?
- Legacy FundedNext Account — no consistency rule once funded (withdrawals use Benchmark Days).
- Flex FundedNext Account — no consistency rule once funded (withdrawals use Benchmark Days).
- Rapid Pro Challenge Account - no consistency rule in the Challenge Phase.
- Rapid Daily — no consistency rule at any stage (withdrawals use the buffer rule).
