*LiveSim Max Withdrawal is the total you can withdraw from a LiveSim account before you are automatically moved to a Live account. It is not a limit on any single withdrawal.
Withdrawals are processed weekly on Wednesdays via Rise or other platforms. Requests must be emailed to the proprietary firm by 2:00 PM CT on the preceding Friday. The minimum net withdrawal amount is $100. Due to regulatory compliance, Rise does not support payouts for traders in certain U.S. territories (Iowa, Minnesota, South Carolina, Guam, Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands) or Haiti.
Withdrawal methods are not optional, and the prop firm retains the right to onboard customers to their payment provider of choice, with some options available only in select regions. Direct crypto withdrawals are only available in select cases.
