Everything you need to know before your first trade
Trading Days
There is no minimum trading day requirement to pass the Qualification Account. You can complete the challenge in as little as one day.
- A trader is not required to trade every day.
- A trading day is counted only when a trader opens at least one trade.
Trading Hours
The trading day starts at 5:00 PM and ends at 4:00 PM the following day, North American Central Time (CT). Weekends and public holidays are not counted as trading days. All positions must be closed by 3:59 PM (CT).
⚠️ Holding positions overnight is strictly prohibited.
Maximum Position Size
The maximum position size depends on the account type you select. Traders may hold several positions at the same time.
Contracts
Standard and micro contracts can be traded simultaneously. One standard contract is equivalent to ten micro contracts.
Trade Copiers & Third-Party Software
