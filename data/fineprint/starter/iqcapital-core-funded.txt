What is a Core Futures Funded Account?
The Core Futures Funded Account is the next stage after passing the challenge and allows you to trade under realistic conditions while qualifying for performance rewards.
Are profits withdrawable?
Yes, profits generated in the Funded Account can be paid out according to the performance reward structure.

Consistency & Profit Target
In the Core Futures Funded Account, the profit target is determined by the 30% consistency rule.
Your best trading day may not exceed 30% of your total profits.
The consistency resets after each performance reward payout and is recalculated based on your next highest profit day.
Example
You start with a fresh account and make:
- Day 1: $300 profit
- Day 2: $270 profit
Your highest profit day determines the required profit target.
→ Best Day: $300
Since this may only represent 30% of the total profit:
300÷ 0.30 = 1000
This means you must reach a total profit of $1,000 for your $300 best day to represent only 30% of total profits and satisfy the consistency rule.
As long as you do not exceed $300 profit in a single day, the target remains unchanged.
​
Drawdown Adjustment After First Payout
Once you receive your first performance reward payout, the Max Drawdown is adjusted to the account's original starting balance of $50,000 or $100,000. From this point on, the drawdown level is fixed.
​
​
What happens if I violate a rule?
If you lose the account through a hard breach, you may start a new funded account by paying a reset fee.
Duration / Inactivity
A Funded Account is valid for 30 days.
You may manually extend it in the dashboard for an additional 30 days for $19.
If no extension is purchased, access to the account will permanently expire.
​
** There is no fixed duration limit for a funded account; however, there is an inactivity rule. At least one trade must be executed within an 8-day period; otherwise, access to the account will be permanently terminated.
