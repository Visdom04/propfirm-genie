Daily Drawdown: What You Need to Know
The Daily Loss Limit per account type and size, what happens when you hit it, and how it is calculated from your previous day's close.
Updated this week
Table of contents
The daily drawdown , also called the Daily Loss Limit (DLL) , is a fixed dollar amount set per account type and size, roughly 1% to 2.5% of your account balance depending on the program. It helps manage risk and ensures responsible trading. This article shows the limit for each account size and explains what happens when you hit it and how it is calculated.
💡 Prefer to trade without a Daily Loss Limit? The No Daily Loss Limit add-on is available at checkout for Elite ACCESS, Elite ACCESS Intraday, Elite Daily and Ignite accounts, for about 30% more than the base price. On Elite ACCESS and Elite ACCESS Intraday, the evaluation price stays the same and the extra is added to the activation fee instead. The add-on can only be chosen during the initial checkout. Learn more: No Daily Loss Limit Add-On .

What happens if you hit the daily drawdown?
Event
What happens
Soft breach
Trading is paused for the rest of the day
Resume trading
You can continue the next day, as long as your account has not exceeded the Max Trailing Drawdown
Session open
When the new trading session starts, account reactivation is not instantaneous. System processing may take up to 5 to 10 minutes before trading is re-enabled
X-Ultra exception
On X-Ultra accounts, reaching the DLL is a hard breach and the account fails immediately. See the X-Ultra Daily Loss Limit (DLL) article
How is it calculated?
The Daily Loss Limit is a dynamic value calculated from your previous day's closing balance . To find your breach level for the current day, subtract the allowed daily loss from that balance.
Example
Value
Daily loss allowed
$625
Previous day's close
$26,000
Breach level for today
$26,000 - $625 = $25,375
If your account's equity drops to $25,375 at any point during the current trading day, the system will automatically execute market orders to liquidate all of your open positions.
