## How GROWTH™ differs from SELECT™ evaluation
**GROWTH™ evaluation is NOT the same as SELECT™ evaluation.** Here are the key differences:
GROWTH™ evaluation has a minimum of 1 trading day, so you can pass immediately. It has no consistency rule. It has a Daily Loss Limit (soft breach). It uses a fixed payout policy, with no choice. SELECT™ evaluation has a minimum of 3 trading days because of its consistency rule. Its consistency rule is 40%, so no single day can be more than 40% of profit. It has no Daily Loss Limit during the evaluation. After passing, you choose the Flex or Daily payout policy.

**In summary:** GROWTH™ evaluation is the fastest way to get funded. You can [pass in just 1 trading day](https://tradeify.co/post/passing-prop-firm-challenges-one-trade-a-day) because there is NO consistency requirement. However, GROWTH™ has a Daily Loss Limit (soft breach) that you must respect. SELECT™ takes longer (minimum 3 days) but gives you more intraday flexibility with no daily loss limit.

For GROWTH™ accounts purchased before September 12, 2025 at 8:00 AM ET, the max position size is 5 contracts (50 micros) on a 50k account, 10 contracts (100 micros) on a 100k account, and 15 contracts (150 micros) on a 150k account.
The GROWTH™ evaluation uses an End of Day Trailing Max Drawdown, which is based on the account balance at the end of the trading day.
For more information on this type of drawdown, see our full article [here](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495897-rules-trailing-max-drawdowns).
GROWTH™ evaluation accounts offer more flexibility during the trading day, because intraday swings don't move the drawdown level. It only updates at the end of the day. The level is still enforced in real time: if your net liquidation value touches it at any moment, the account fails immediately, even if you recover before the close.
There is no activation fee for GROWTH™ Simulated Funded accounts, and the Simulated Funded counterpart cannot be reset.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Does**GROWTH™ evaluation**have a consistency rule?**
A: No. GROWTH™ evaluation does NOT have a consistency requirement. You can pass in as few as 1 trading day by hitting the profit target. SELECT™ evaluation requires 40% consistency and takes a minimum of 3 trading days.
**Q: Does**GROWTH™ evaluation**have a Daily Loss Limit?**
A: Yes. GROWTH™ evaluation DOES have a Daily Loss Limit (soft breach): $600 (25K), $1,250 (50K), $2,500 (100K), $3,750 (150K). If you hit it, trading stops for the day but your account is not failed. SELECT™ evaluation has NO daily loss limit during the evaluation phase.
**Q: Can I pass**GROWTH™ evaluation **in one day?**
A: Yes. Because there is no consistency requirement, you can pass in 1 trading day. Hit the profit target while staying within the drawdown and daily loss limits. SELECT™ requires a minimum of 3 trading days due to its 40% consistency rule.
**Q: What's the difference between GROWTH™ and SELECT™ evaluation?**
A: (1) GROWTH™ can pass in 1 day with no consistency rule, while SELECT™ requires 3+ days due to 40% consistency. (2) GROWTH™ HAS a daily loss limit (soft breach), while SELECT™ has NO daily loss limit during evaluation. (3) GROWTH™ has a fixed payout policy, while SELECT™ lets you choose Flex or Daily AFTER passing. See the comparison table at the top of this article.
**Q: Can I choose my payout policy on GROWTH™ like I can with SELECT™?**
A: No. GROWTH™ accounts use a fixed payout structure. With SELECT™, you choose Flex (5-Day) or Daily payouts after passing. If you want that choice, consider SELECT™ evaluation instead.
**Q: Is there a time limit to complete GROWTH™ evaluation?**
A: No. There is no deadline to hit the profit target, as long as you don't breach the trailing drawdown. However, the weekly inactivity rule still applies to evaluations: you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday) on each evaluation account, or the account can be closed for inactivity after an email warning. The same applies to SELECT™ evaluation.
