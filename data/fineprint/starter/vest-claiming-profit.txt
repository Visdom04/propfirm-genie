## Claiming Profit
Claim Profit moves profit from a **Live Funded Account** to your **Primary Account**. It's the first of two steps if you want to move funds all the way out to an external wallet.
**Claim Profit is not the same as a Withdrawal.** Claim Profit moves funds from a Funded Account to your Primary Account. Withdrawal moves funds from your Primary Account to an external wallet. See [Withdrawals](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/platform/withdrawals) for that second step.
This flow works identically whether your Live Funded Account came from a passed evaluation or an Instant Account purchase.
* * *
### How to Claim Profit[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#how-to-claim-profit)
### Submit a Claim Profit request[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#submit-a-claim-profit-request)
From your Live Funded Account Portfolio page, submit a Claim Profit request. You may need to close open positions or orders first. Your profit split, 80% or 90% with evaluation, 95% flat on Instant, whichever applies to your account, will be credited to your Primary Account in 24 hours.
### Funds arrive in your Primary Account[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#funds-arrive-in-your-primary-account)
Once processed, the USDC is credited to your Primary Account. From here, it's yours to trade with, hold, or withdraw.
### Withdraw to an external wallet (optional)[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#withdraw-to-an-external-wallet-optional)
If you want funds outside Vest entirely, withdraw USDC from your Primary Account to any wallet address on a supported chain. See [Withdrawals](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/platform/withdrawals#how-to-withdraw) for the full flow.
* * *
### Profit Split[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#profit-split)
Your rate depends on your account type: **80% or 90% with evaluation** (whichever you chose at purchase) or **95% flat on Instant**. It's set at purchase and does not change when your account goes Live. Applied flat at the point of every claim, with no further fees or deductions.
* * *
### Processing Time & Frequency[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/claiming-profit#processing-time-and-frequency)
* **24 hours** for every claim, regardless of how old the account is.
* **Irrevocable once initiated,** a Claim Profit request cannot be reversed.
* Claim as often as you like. There's no cap on frequency and no cap on the amount you can claim.
