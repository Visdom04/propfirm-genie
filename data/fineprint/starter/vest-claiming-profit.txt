Claiming Profit
Claim Profit moves profit from a Live Funded Account to your Primary Account . It's the first of two steps if you want to move funds all the way out to an external wallet.
Claim Profit is not the same as a Withdrawal. Claim Profit moves funds from a Funded Account to your Primary Account. Withdrawal moves funds from your Primary Account to an external wallet. See Withdrawals for that second step.
This flow works identically whether your Live Funded Account came from a passed evaluation or an Instant Account purchase.
How to Claim Profit
1
Submit a Claim Profit request
From your Live Funded Account Portfolio page, submit a Claim Profit request. You may need to close open positions or orders first.
Your profit split, 80% or 90% with evaluation, 95% flat on Instant, whichever applies to your account, will be credited to your Primary Account in 24 hours.
2
Funds arrive in your Primary Account
Once processed, the USDC is credited to your Primary Account. From here, it's yours to trade with, hold, or withdraw.
3
Withdraw to an external wallet (optional)
If you want funds outside Vest entirely, withdraw USDC from your Primary Account to any wallet address on a supported chain. See Withdrawals for the full flow.
Profit Split
Your rate depends on your account type: 80% or 90% with evaluation (whichever you chose at purchase) or 95% flat on Instant . It's set at purchase and does not change when your account goes Live. Applied flat at the point of every claim, with no further fees or deductions.
Processing Time & Frequency
-
24 hours for every claim, regardless of how old the account is.
-
Irrevocable once initiated, a Claim Profit request cannot be reversed.
-
Claim as often as you like. There's no cap on frequency and no cap on the amount you can claim.
