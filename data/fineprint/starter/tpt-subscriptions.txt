Your **Trading Test** with TakeProfitTrader is structured as a **monthly subscription**. This approach provides you with complete flexibility to trade at your own pace — there is **no time limit** to reach your profit target.
## S ubscription Billing
* You will be charged a subscription fee **each month on the same calendar day as the original purchase of the first account under this subscription**, unless the test is cancelled.
* If you **break a rule**, your subscription will **not cancel automatically**. You must manually cancel it from your **dashboard** at [takeprofittrader.com](https://takeprofittrader.com/).
* You also have the option to **reset your test account** at any time for a fee. This will not change your **subscription renewal date**.
## Overdue Subscriptions
Overdue subscriptions will be marked with a**red card symbol** in your user dashboard, with the note, “**Subscription balance is overdue. The account cannot move to PRO unless the payment issue is resolved.”**
![Image 2](https://takeprofittraderhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/36968449433757)
**Subscription renewals are fully automated.** If your renewal payment fails, we will attempt to charge your card again.
Please make sure to have a sufficient balance and valid payment method on file because**if the final payment attempt fails, your account will expire.**We are not able to reactivate expired accounts.
* **Note:** As stated above, if you pass the test with an overdue subscription, your account will not move to PRO until the balance is paid. If all renewal attempts are unsuccessful, the account will not move to PRO.
If you need to **update your payment method**, you can follow these steps:[Changing Your Payment Method](https://takeprofittraderhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15172807922589-Changing-Your-Payment-Method).
## T ransition to PRO Account
Once you **successfully pass your trading test**, your **subscription will be automatically canceled**.
There are **no recurring subscription fees** for **PRO accounts** — they operate differently from test subscriptions.
