Loss Limits
Vest Capital's loss limits follow your account type , not its phase. Whatever applies during the Evaluation Phase carries through unchanged once you're Live.
Every Funded Account carries Max. Drawdown. Most accounts with evaluation also carry a Daily Loss Limit, selected at purchase. Instant Accounts, and the evaluation-path "No Daily Loss Limit" option, carry Max. Drawdown only.
Daily Loss Limit (DLL) at a glance
If your account has a DLL, the daily reset boundary is 20:00 ET . The reset balance includes reserved collateral but excludes unrealized PnL . Your daily floor is reset balance × (1 − your DLL rate) + net non-trading transfers since reset . Current account equity, including unrealized PnL, is checked against this floor.
For example, a $25,000 reset balance with a 4% DLL sets a $24,000 daily floor before transfers. Withdrawals lower this floor by the full amount; non-trading credits raise it by the full amount. Equity falling below either applicable loss floor closes the account permanently. The separate Max. Drawdown floor still applies. Instant Accounts and the “No Daily Loss Limit” option have no DLL.
Breaching either rule results in immediate, permanent account closure. There is no warning and no grace period. You would need to purchase a new Funded Account to trade again.

Daily Loss Limit
Selected at purchase , on the evaluation path only. 3%, 4%, or none. If chosen, it applies during the Evaluation Phase and continues unchanged once you're Live. Never applies on Instant Accounts, and not present at all if you chose "none."
-
The daily loss allowance is 3% or 4% of your reset balance: account balance plus reserved collateral, excluding unrealized PnL.
-
The daily reset boundary is 20:00 ET (America/New_York). The reset balance is captured on the first account update after that boundary, before that update is applied.
-
Daily floor = reset balance × (1 − rate) + net non-trading transfers since reset. Before transfers, this is 97% of reset balance at 3%, or 96% at 4%.
-
Between resets, trading PnL does not move the floor. Non-trading transfers adjust it dollar-for-dollar: withdrawals lower it by the full amount, while credits raise it by the full amount. Current equity includes unrealized PnL and is checked against the adjusted floor.
If your account equity drops through that day's threshold at any point before the next reset, the account closes immediately and permanently.
Example
A Platinum account with Evaluation, $25,000 capital, 4% Daily Loss Limit chosen at purchase.
Max. Drawdown is fixed at $23,500 as an equity floor from day one: $25,000 minus the $1,500 loss allowance (6% of starting capital). The floor never moves, regardless of how the account performs.
Daily Loss Limit recalculates every day. If your reset balance is $25,000, the floor is $25,000 × 0.96 = $24,000 before transfers. An open position with $2,000 of unrealized profit raises current equity to $27,000 but does not raise the reset balance or the daily floor. If your realized balance, including reserved collateral, is $27,000 at the next reset, the new floor is $27,000 × 0.96 = $25,920.
A $500 withdrawal after a $25,000 reset lowers the DLL floor from $24,000 to $23,500. The full withdrawal is deducted from the floor, even if the account has made profits since reset. A $500 non-trading credit instead raises it to $24,500. The separate Max. Drawdown floor stays fixed.
Both limits are active at once. Before transfers in this example, the Daily Loss Limit floor ($24,000) sits above the Max. Drawdown floor ($23,500). Equity falling below either applicable floor closes the account; equality alone does not breach a limit.
An Instant Account or a "No Daily Loss Limit" evaluation phase account only has the Max. Drawdown floor to watch. There's no moving threshold to track.
What Happens If You Breach
The account closes immediately and permanently. Whichever rule was breached, and whether it happened during the Evaluation Phase or on a Live Funded Account. There is no warning, no grace period, and the account cannot be recovered.
Checking Your Risk Before You Trade
The order form shows an estimated Fail Price for both long and short exposure. What it references depends on your account:
-
Instant Accounts, and evaluation accounts with "No Daily Loss Limit", Fail Price shows the price that would put you at your Max. Drawdown floor.
-
Other evaluation accounts , Fail Price shows the price that would trigger Max. Drawdown or the Daily Loss Limit, whichever comes first.
Use estimated Fail Price to check your downside before submitting a trade. See Order Types for more.
