How can I withdraw my Performance Reward?
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Table of contents
TL;DR: To withdraw your Performance Reward , confirm you're eligible, transfer your profit to your wallet from the Account Overview page, check your account model's cycle/consistency rule/Benchmark Day (or buffer) rules, then complete the withdrawal from the "Payout" section using your preferred method. The minimum withdrawal is $250 , and withdrawals can't be requested before the End of Day once you've started trading that day.
To withdraw a Performance Reward from your FundedNext Account , follow these steps.
Step 1: Check your eligibility
Make sure you meet the Performance Reward eligibility criteria for your model:
🔗 Legacy FundedNext Account
🔗 Flex FundedNext Account
🔗 Rapid Pro FundedNext Account
🔗 Rapid Daily FundedNext Account
🔗 Direct FundedNext Account
​
Step 2: Transfer profit to your wallet
- From the "Account Overview" page, click "Proceed to Withdraw" if you're eligible.
- Enter the amount you want to transfer — it must be at least $250 .
- Click "Transfer Wallet" to move the amount to the "Payout" section.
Note: For Legacy and Flex FundedNext Accounts, if your total profit is less than $500 , the "Proceed to Withdraw" button stays locked until you meet the minimum.
Step 3: Check your model's withdrawal rules
Legacy FundedNext Account:
- With 30 or fewer Benchmark Days , you can withdraw up to 50% of your simulated profit , capped at 3,000(25K) or 6,000(50K and $100K) .
- After 30 Benchmark Days , you can withdraw 100% — both the 50% limit and the cap are removed. (Which will breach the account)
- Reward Share: 80%.
Flex FundedNext Account:
- After 5 Benchmark Days , you can withdraw up to 50% of your simulated profit (minimum $250), capped at 1,500(50K) , 2,500(100K) , or 4,000(150K) .
- After 5 withdrawal the account gets concluded.
- You can move toward live trading as early as 1 5 withdrawals from Flex Account.
- Reward Share: 95%.
Rapid Pro FundedNext Account:
- Once you meet the 40% Consistency Rule and have $500 profit in the cycle, you can withdraw a maximum of 800(25K) , 1,200(50K) , or 2,500(100K) .
- Reward Share: 90%.
Rapid Daily FundedNext Account:
- Once your EOD balance reaches the buffer level ( $26,100 / $52,100 / $102,600 ) with $500 profit in the cycle, any profit above the buffer is withdrawable, up to 800(25K) , 1,200(50K) , or 2,500(100K) .
- Reward Share: 90%.
Direct FundedNext Account:
- Once you meet the 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule
- Your EOD balance reaches the buffer level ( $52,100 )
- $800 profit in the cycle, you can withdraw a maximum of 1,200 .
- Reward Share: 90%.
If you request more than your model's maximum, you'll receive an error. Once you've started trading on a given day, no withdrawal can be requested before the End of Day (EOD).
Step 4: Withdraw from the "Payout" section
After transferring to your wallet, complete your withdrawal from the "Payout" section by selecting your preferred withdrawal method.
What are the necessary adjustments after the withdrawal of the first Performance Reward?
- If this is your first Performance Reward, your maximum loss limit will be set to your initial account balance. For example, if your account size is $50,000, after the withdrawal of your Performance Reward (Rapid & Legacy), the Maximum Loss Limit will also lock to $50,000.
- For the Bolt FundedNext Account, the maximum loss limit will be set to $50,100 after the first withdrawal of the Performance Reward.
- For the Flex FundedNext Account, the maximum loss limit will be set to $50,100, $100,100 & $150,100 after the first withdrawal of the Performance Reward.
- For Rapid Pro and Daily FundedNext Account, the maximum loss limit will be set to $25,100, $50,100 & $100,100 after the first withdrawal of the Performance Reward.
- For the Direct Account, the maximum loss limit will be set to $50,100 after the first withdrawal of the Performance Reward.
Note: Effective 10 July 2026, Rapid and Bolt accounts will no longer be available for new purchases or account resets.
