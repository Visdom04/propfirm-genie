All Collections General Market Data, Add-Ons and Fees Market Data, Add-Ons and Fees
Where your market data comes from, non-professional status, Rithmic add-ons and their prices, and other fees.
Updated over a week ago
Copy for LLM
Your accounts run on Rithmic, which provides the market data and order routing. Onyx and other platforms are platforms only: they display Rithmic's data but aren't the data provider.
Market data and professional status
When you sign your Rithmic agreements, choose Non-Professional . Professionals (people with Series licenses or registered with the SEC, NFA or another regulator) can't take part in our simulated programs, including S2L.

- Buy add-ons from the Product Add-ons tab on your Dashboard.
- They expire at 5:00 PM ET on the last day of the month, whatever day you buy them, so buy on the 1st for full value.
- They don't renew automatically and aren't refundable.
- You need Market Depth for full Level 2 data on any platform.
Other fees
Pro Account activation is $5. Commissions are charged per contract and are included in your P&L. Onyx and Quantower licenses are included on simulated accounts; other platforms need their own license.
