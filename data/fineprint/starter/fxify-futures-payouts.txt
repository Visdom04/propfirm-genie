At FXIFY Futures, we maintain a transparent and structured payout process for our traders. Our policy includes buffer zone limits, tier-based withdrawal payouts, payout caps, and clear procedures to ensure fairness, discipline, and platform sustainability.
Buffer Zone Limits
The payout buffer zone for both Standard and Expert plans is as follows:
Standard Accounts
- $50,000 Account → $500
- $100,000 Account → $1,000
- $150,000 Account → $1,500
Expert Accounts
- $50,000 Account → $1,000
- $100,000 Account → $1,500
- $150,000 Account → $2,000
Tier-Based Withdrawal Payouts
Withdrawals follow a tier-based structure, where the percentage of profits available for withdrawal increases with each payout. This applies to both Standard and Expert accounts:
- Payout 1 → 60% of the amount above the buffer zone
- Payout 2 → 70% of the amount above the buffer zone
- Payout 3 → 80% of the amount above the buffer zone
- Payout 4 → 90% of the amount above the buffer zone
- Payout 5 and onwards → 100% of the amount above the buffer zone
Key Conditions
- Payout Requests : Can be made every 14 calendar days from the first trade on your funded account.
- Profit Requirement : Payouts can only be requested if profits exceed the buffer zone by at least $100.
- Consistency Rule:
- Standard accounts: 30%
- Expert accounts: 40%
The consistency rule must be met before a payout request can be made. Consistency calculations reset after each payout request and are based only on profits generated since the last payout.
- Buffer Zone Lock-In : The buffer zone amount locks in as your new max drawdown limit, which will be fixed at the initial balance.
- Max Drawdown : After each withdrawal, the max drawdown locks at the account’s starting balance.
Payout Caps for Standard and Expert Accounts
To support sustainable risk management and platform liquidity, FXIFY Futures has payout caps based on account size:
- $50,000 Account → Maximum withdrawable amount: $5,000
- $100,000 Account → Maximum withdrawable amount: $10,000
- $150,000 Account → Maximum withdrawable amount: $15,000
Maximum Payouts per Account:
- The payout cap on each account refers to the total payouts received on that account, not just the profits made.
- Once the cap is reached, the account will enter review by our trading team .
- In some cases, accounts may be transitioned to live infrastructure before the payout cap is reached, at the team’s discretion.
- To be considered for transition, the account must be active at the time of review.
After Review:
- The trading team will determine if your account qualifies to move into a live trading environment.
- To be considered for transition, the account must be active at the time of review.
- If the account does not qualify for the transition to live, it will be closed, and a 30-day waiting period may apply before you can repurchase a new evaluation account.
- If the account doesn’t qualify for transition to a live funded account and that account is closed, it will not impact other Sim Funded accounts if a trader has more than one.
Example
Payout Above the Buffer Zone
- Account: $100,000 Standard
- Balance: $102,500
- Buffer Zone: $1,000
- Withdrawable Profit: $1,500
Payout 1 (60%) → $900
​ Profit Split (80%) → $720 to trader
​ Remaining Balance → $101,600

This structured payout policy ensures fairness, disciplined trading, and liquidity protection while offering traders a clear growth path. For further questions, please contact our support team at support@fxifyfutures.com .
