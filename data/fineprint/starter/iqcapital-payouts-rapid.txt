IQ Capital's Rapid plan for Futures accounts offers two variants: 1D (daily payout eligibility) and 3D (payout every 3 days, 40% consistency requirement, no Daily Loss Limit, larger payout caps).
​
​
When can I request a Performance Reward?
A Performance Reward can only be requested once all of the following conditions are met:
- At least 1 active trading day (1D) / at least 3 active trading days per cycle (3D)
- Must comply with the Scalping Rule
- 1D only: Maximum one violation of the Daily Loss Limit
- 3D only: No Daily Loss Limit applies
A maximum of 5 payouts can be requested; after that, the account will be closed.

What counts as an active trading day?
- At least one trade has been placed.
- There is no minimum profit requirement per trading day.
​
When does a Trading Day end?
The trading day ends daily at 4:45 PM (EDT) / 10:45 PM (CET Berlin). Performance rewards can be claimed after the first active trading day (1D) or after the third active trading day of the cycle (3D).
​
​
Drawdown adjustment after the first payout
After the first Performance Reward, the End-of-Day (EOD) drawdown is adjusted to the starting balance and remains fixed at that level permanently. This applies to both 1D and 3D.

Can I withdraw all my profits at once?
For each Performance Reward request, a maximum of 50% of the total profits can be withdrawn, subject to the payout caps above. The remaining profits stay in the account as a trading buffer. This applies to both 1D and 3D.
Example (100K Account – First Performance Reward, 1D)
- Total profit: $6,000
- Maximum payout (50% rule): $1,500
Although 50% of $6,000 equals $3,000, the payout is capped at $1,500 because this is the maximum allowed for Reward 1–2 on a $100,000 Account under 1D. The remaining profits stay in the account as a trading buffer.
​
​
Example (100K Account – First Performance Reward, 3D)
- Total profit: $6,000
- Maximum payout (50% rule): $2,000
Although 50% of $6,000 equals $3,000, the payout is capped at $2,000 because this is the maximum allowed for Reward 1–2 on a $100,000 Account under 3D. The remaining profits stay in the account as a trading buffer.
​
​
After the first Performance Reward
For every subsequent Performance Reward, at least 50% new profits must be generated since the last payout. This applies to both 1D and 3D.
​
Example 1
- Balance after payout: $102,000
- New Performance Reward request: $1,000
- Requirement: At least $500 in new profits since the last payout
Result: Eligible for payout.
​
Example 2
- Balance after payout: $105,000
- New Performance Reward request: $1,500
- Requirement: At least $750 in new profits since the last payout
Result: Eligible for payout.
​
​
What happens to consistency after a Performance Reward?
After each Performance Reward, consistency is fully reset. It is then recalculated based on the best trading day within the new trading cycle. Under 3D, the 40% consistency requirement applies to each new cycle.
​
​
Profit Split Structure
From the very first $1 in profit:
- 80% goes to the trader
- 20% goes to the company
This provides a consistent 80/20 profit split with no tiered structure, for both 1D and 3D.
​
Example If you generate $2,000 in total profit:
- You request a 50% payout ($1,000)
- The 80/20 split is applied to the payout amount.
Result:
- Trader: $800
- Company: $200
Payout methods
Performance Rewards are available via:
- Crypto (USDC – ERC-20)
- Bank Transfer
