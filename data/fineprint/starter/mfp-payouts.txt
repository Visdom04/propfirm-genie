Payouts
Copy page Copy page
Read funded payout terms, complete Rise onboarding, clear eligibility gates, submit a request, and understand its effect on the account.
Copy page Copy page
Payouts are available from the
Payouts panel for an active
funded account. Select the intended account before reviewing or submitting a
request.
​
Payout request availability
Payout requests opened on September 11, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Daylight
Time (04:00 UTC) for all existing and newly funded accounts. Every request
must meet the normal payout requirements below.
​
Daily payout window
Each funded account can submit one payout request per day. Elite accounts are
the exception: they have no daily limit, so you can request again as soon as
your previous request finishes. Checkout shows Elite payout frequency as
On Demand . See Elite Accounts .
On every other plan, the allowance resets
at 12:00 a.m. (midnight) New York time ( America/New_York ), the same time
as the daily loss reset .
This is 04:00 UTC during Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) and 05:00 UTC during
Eastern Standard Time (EST) .
The allowance follows calendar days, rather than a rolling 24-hour wait from
your previous request. The payout panel shows the next window in your local
time and in Eastern time. Two funded accounts held by the same trader each
have their own daily allowance.
Midnight renews the request allowance; it does not approve or pay an existing
request. A pending request must finish and all eligibility gates must be clear
before you can submit another one.
Submitted requests cannot be cancelled. If a request is rejected, that day’s
allowance is restored, so you can submit a new request right away once all
eligibility gates are clear. Every request is subject to review and approval.
Daily or on-demand request availability does not guarantee immediate payment;
review and delivery can take longer than the request window.
​
Minimum and profit split
On Select, Prime, and Signature, the minimum is $100 of gross profit before
the split . This pays $80 with an 80/20 split or $90 with a 90/10 split. The request form accepts your net share
and shows the gross account deduction before you confirm.
$1K and $2.5K Select prize accounts use their own terms: a 90/10 profit split
and a minimum request of $30 on $1K or $50 on $2.5K. Payout Terms on
the account shows the split and minimum that apply.
Elite accounts use an 80/20 split, or 90/10 with the upgrade, and a minimum of
1% of the account size before the split: $50 on $5K, $100 on $10K,
$250 on $25K, and $500 on $50K ($40, $80, $200, and $400 to you
on 80/20, or $45, $90, $225, and $450 on 90/10). A $1K Elite prize
account has a $30 minimum before the split ($24 to you). See
Elite Accounts .
Select, Prime, Signature, and Elite have no minimum trading-day or
qualifying-day requirement, consistency gate, or payout count cap per account.
The lifetime payout cap below still applies.
​
Lifetime payout cap
Each user can receive at most $100,000 in total lifetime payouts across
all MyFundedPerps accounts. The cap is per user, combined across every
account and model type (Select, Prime, Signature, and Elite), not per account.
The cap counts the payout amount you actually receive after the profit split,
not the gross amount deducted from the account. Purchasing new accounts or
resetting existing accounts does not reset it.
The lifetime cap is separate from the per-request rules on this page. The
plan’s gross minimum and, outside Elite, the daily request allowance still
apply to every request, and staying within them does not raise the lifetime cap. It is also
separate from the $100K funded allocation limit per model type, which limits
the starting size of your active funded accounts rather than the money you
receive.
The Payout Split Calculator
can help you compare hypothetical amounts. Its fee-refund option does not
promise a refund or confirm payout eligibility. Read the
calculator guide
for an example and its limits.
​
Complete Rise onboarding
Only payouts with a cash portion need Rise. If you choose 100% store credit,
you can submit without a Rise account or invitation. Store-credit-only accounts
skip Rise setup entirely.
For cash delivery, enter the email you want paid to, send the
invitation, then accept it and create the Rise account. The invitation email
comes from Rise after you select Send Invitation . Check your inbox and
spam folder, then follow the invitation to complete setup. The panel shows
when setup is accepted or needs attention. If the invitation could not be
completed, contact support from the panel.
Check the email carefully. Approved payouts are delivered to the connected
Rise account. While Streamer Mode is on, the Rise
email is blurred everywhere it appears, including the confirmation after the
invitation is sent and the payout panel once Rise is connected. Press and hold
Hold to peek next to the Rise Email field to check the address before you
send the invitation.
​
Clear the payout gates
The request form explains the first condition that is not yet satisfied.
Before requesting:
- keep the funded account active;
- wait for any pending request to finish and, except on Elite, for the next
daily window;
- close every open position;
- cancel resting orders and wait for working orders to settle;
- have at least the plan’s minimum in available gross profit in the current
cycle ($100 on Select, Prime, and Signature).
Payouts use realized balance. Close positions and settle orders before relying
on the displayed request amount.
​
Understand the amount
The requested amount is your profit-split share. The confirmation shows both
your requested amount and the gross amount deducted from the funded account.
New payout requests use whole cents. Max rounds your available share down
to the nearest cent. If you enter more than two decimal places, the submitted
amount rounds to the nearest cent; an exact half-cent rounds to the even cent.
For example, $330.108976 becomes $330.11. The rounded request must still fit
within your available share.
On approval, the full gross amount leaves the account balance and a new payout
cycle starts. Any undistributed profit retained below that new cycle baseline
is a balance buffer rather than available profit for another withdrawal. This
retained buffer cannot be withdrawn in later cycles. Elite accounts are the
exception: profit you leave in an Elite account stays available for a later
payout. See Elite Accounts .
​
Permanent max loss limit after the first withdrawal
This lock applies to Select, Prime, and Signature. Elite accounts have no
lock: their MLL floor never moves, including after withdrawals. See
Elite Accounts .
Before the first withdrawal, the max loss limit (MLL) is starting balance minus
the plan’s static drawdown allowance. On the first withdrawal it becomes
exactly the starting balance , permanently. Later withdrawals never lower
or raise it. You receive an in-app notice and an email explaining the lock.
For example, a $100K Signature account starts with an MLL floor of $94,000.
After its first withdrawal the floor is $100,000. Equity at $100,000 is allowed;
equity at $99,999 breaches and closes the account. Equity includes open-position
profit or loss. Withdrawing $3,200 at an 80/20 split deducts $4,000 gross from
the account, so withdrawing all $4,000 profit leaves no loss buffer above the
locked floor.
The 3% daily loss rule continues to apply. The dashboard shows both loss floors.
Check them before opening another position.
Before submitting, review Balance After Payout , Max Loss Floor After
Payout , and Loss Buffer After Payout directly below the amount field.
Elite requests show the gross deduction and Balance After Payout only,
because their floor does not change.
The preview updates as you change the requested amount and appears again in
confirmation. The loss buffer is the projected balance minus the permanent
max loss floor, using the full gross deduction rather than just your share.
A zero-buffer warning appears when the payout leaves no room above the floor.
If the projected balance falls below the floor, an explicit breach warning
appears and submission is blocked, including when the entered amount exceeds
the available payout. Reduce your request to retain room for trading losses
and fees. This buffer
only measures the max loss floor; the daily loss rule still applies. Cash and
store-credit allocation do not change the buffer.
​
Daily loss limit after a withdrawal
A withdrawal never counts as a daily loss. On plans with a daily loss limit,
a payout first uses up any profit you made earlier in the same trading day.
That withdrawn profit no longer adds room under the daily loss limit, so the
daily loss floor never drops below where it started that day.
For example, a $5K account with a 3% daily loss limit ($150) starts the day
at $5,000, so its daily loss floor is $4,850. It gains $280, then withdraws
$280 gross. The balance returns to $5,000 and the daily loss floor stays at
$4,850. If the withdrawal is larger than the day’s profit, the floor moves
down only by the extra amount, so the withdrawal still does not count as a
loss.
​
Split cash and store credit
Yes, funded payouts can still be redeemed for store credit. On most
accounts you can take 100% as cash through Rise, 100% as store credit, or any
split between the two.
Use the slider, a preset, or enter the store-credit amount. The request panel
shows each part before you confirm.
If you split a payout, the cash part must be at least $1.00, the smallest
amount Rise can send. To take less than $1.00 as cash, convert the whole
request to store credit instead.
Some accounts are paid in store credit only. $1K and $2.5K Select accounts,
for example, cannot take any part of a payout as cash. On these accounts the request panel
shows Paid as Store Credit in place of the slider, and the whole requested
amount is converted to store credit. Payout Terms lists the payout method as
Store credit only . These payouts do not receive the store-credit boost
because the accounts already have a 90/10 split, so a $30 request grants $30
in store credit. Eligible payouts from these accounts are automatically approved
when you submit, and the store credit is added immediately. The gross account
deduction and new payout cycle also take effect immediately. Requests that need
verification or have unresolved trading reviews remain pending for review.
On other accounts, the store-credit portion receives a 5% boost. For example, converting $100 of
a payout grants $105 in store credit. The boosted credit cannot be withdrawn
as cash. It is added when the payout is paid, while any cash portion is sent
through Rise.
Like all store credit, it expires 45 days after it is added. See
Store Credit Expiry .
The full requested amount still counts toward the payout-cycle deduction. The
boost increases the store-credit value you receive; it does not increase the
amount deducted from the funded account.
If you chose store credit by mistake, contact support before spending any of
that credit.
​
Submit and wait for review
Eligible $1K and $2.5K Select store-credit-only payouts complete on submission.
Other payout requests go through review. Requests may be reviewed
automatically or by staff. Frequent requests can take longer to review.
Requests submitted between 12:00 AM and 9:00 AM ET can also take a few extra
hours. If one is still pending after a few minutes, the request panel shows
when to expect an update. While a request is under review,
trading on that funded account is locked. Do not submit another request or try
to change the supporting balance.
Payout History shows the requested amount, cash and boosted store-credit parts,
request and decision dates, current status, and the gross deduction for a paid
payout. A paid payout can also show a certificate action. To earn a case by
sharing a paid payout, use
Share to Claim
on Rewards.
Eligibility is checked again when the request is submitted. If account state
changed, review the updated blocker and try again only after it is resolved.
