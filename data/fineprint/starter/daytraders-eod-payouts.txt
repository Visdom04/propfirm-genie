EOD Pro Account payouts: eligibility, balance limits by size and how to request.
Updated over a week ago
Copy for LLM
EOD Pro Accounts have a 100% split of eligible simulated profit. Requests start at $500 , in $50 increments.
Eligibility
- 8 Qualifying Days since your last payout or activation. The day you request doesn't count.
- Your best day is no more than 30% of the cycle's profit.
- Your balance is at or above the minimum to request, and stays at or above the minimum after the payout.
- A Payout Method is set up (Plane or DT Wallet).

Requesting a payout
On your Dashboard, click Request Payout , choose the account and amount, and submit. Place at least one trade afterwards so the payout shows on your charts. Payouts approved before the daily reports load (5:00–6:00 PM ET) are reflected the same day.
If you're called to live: your simulated accounts are closed. Excess simulated balances are generally forfeited, but they may be used as performance rewards in live, subject to the Risk Team's discretion. If you are not comfortable with this, do not build large excess simulated balances.
