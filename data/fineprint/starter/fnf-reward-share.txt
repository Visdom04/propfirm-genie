What will be my Reward Share from the Futures FundedNext Account?
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Table of contents
TL;DR: After receiving a FundedNext Account of any model and size, traders are eligible for a Reward Share up to 95% based on the account model and on the withdrawn portion of their account growth. The Performance Reward is calculated based on the approved withdrawal amount. 90% for Rapid Pro & Daily and Direct, and 95% for the Flex. Challenges subject to the applicable withdrawal limits and rules .
For the Futures Flex Account , traders get a 95% Reward Share , allowing them to retain a higher portion of their profits.
After receiving a Rapid, Legacy , or Bolt FundedNext Account , traders become eligible for an 80% Reward Share on their account growth.
For the Futures Direct Account , traders receive a 90% Reward Share from the beginning.
In Rapid Pro and Daily FundedNext Account , traders will receive a 90% Reward Share from the beginning.
Example 1:
A trader with a $25K Legacy FundedNext Account makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $1,500 before 30 Benchmark Days (the 50% amount, which is under the cap).
Account balance after withdrawal: $26,500
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $1,500 × 80% = $1,200
Example 2:
A trader with a $25K Rapid FundedNext Account makes $1,500 growth and decides to withdraw $800 (maximum withdrawal amount).
Account balance after withdrawal: $25,700
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $800 × 80% = $640
Example 3:
A trader with a $50K Bolt FundedNext Account makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $1,200 (maximum withdrawal amount).
Account balance after withdrawal: $51,800
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $1,200 × 80% = $960
Example 4:
A trader with a $50K Flex FundedNext Account with a 95% Reward Share makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $1,500 (the 50% amount, which is under the limit).
Account balance after withdrawal: $51,500
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $1,500 × 95% = $1,425
Example 5:
A trader with a $50K Rapid Pro FundedNext Account makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $1,200 (maximum withdrawal limit).
Account balance after withdrawal: $51,800
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $1,200 × 90% = $1,080
Example 6:
A trader with a $50K Rapid Daily FundedNext Account makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $900 (above the buffer).
Account balance after withdrawal: $52,100
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $900 × 90% = $810
Example 7:
A trader with a $50K Direct Account makes $3,000 growth and decides to withdraw $900 (above the buffer).
Account balance after withdrawal: $52,100
Reward Share applied on withdrawal: $900 × 90% = $810
