How do the Growth and Pro products differ?
How do the Growth and Pro products differ?
FTMO Futures offers two products that share the same single-phase Evaluation and the same 90/10 Payout Ratio , but differ in their conditions, risk rules, and pricing, so you can choose what fits your trading style. Both are available in 50K , 100K , and 150K sizes.
Both products use an End of Day (EOD) Trailing Max Drawdown and a 90/10 Payout Ratio at the Sim-Funded Account stage; the main differences are described below in the informative summary. To familiarise yourself with the full payout conditions and trading rules, see the Trading Objectives and Rules page.
Growth
The lower-priced option compared to the Pro Evaluation of the same size. It has no Daily Loss Limit during the Evaluation and a soft Daily Loss Limit on the Sim-Funded Account, so a daily breach does not automatically end the account. In each payout cycle, you can request a payout of up to 50% of the available profit in your Sim-Funded Account. The remaining profit stays in the account.
Pro
Built for experienced, disciplined traders. It uses a tighter, more demanding rule set with a hard Daily Loss Limit , in exchange for a better Profit Target-to-Max Drawdown ratio, higher payout caps, and full payout withdrawal in a single cycle. In each payout cycle, you can request a payout of up to 100% of the available profit in your Sim-Funded Account.
