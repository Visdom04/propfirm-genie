What is the current fee schedule in E2T evaluations and on funded trading accounts?
- During the Trader Career Path® / Gauntlet Mini™ evaluations:
- If you have non-professional CME status: Data fees are covered in the evaluation cost.
- If you have professional CME status: Data fee cost is $140.00/month per exchange.
- Once you pass the evaluation:
- On a LiveSim® Account:
- If you have non-professional CME status: One-time per account activation fee of $139.00, covering all four CME exchanges*.
- If you have professional CME status: Data fee cost is $140.00/month per exchange.
- On a Live Account: Data fee cost is $140.00/month per exchange for Rithmic users, and $156.00/month per exchange for NinjaTrader Live account holders.
