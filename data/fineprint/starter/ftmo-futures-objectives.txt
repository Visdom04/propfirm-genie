Evaluation
The Evaluation is the first phase of your FTMO Futures journey. To pass the Evaluation and have the option to advance to a Sim-Funded Account, you must demonstrate consistent, rules-compliant trading and meet all the required Trading Objectives concurrently. Accounts are assessed for eligibility to pass after the end of the trading day.
For the purposes of Trading Objectives and other rules and conditions, a trading day is defined as the period beginning at 6:00 p.m. ET and ending at 4:10 p.m. ET on the following calendar day.
Trading Objectives
Profit Target
$3,000
To complete the Profit Target , you must achieve a defined minimum profit above the Initial Simulated Capital on the account.
You will meet this objective once your account balance exceeds the Initial Simulated Capital by the required Profit Target, with all positions closed.
The Profit Target is $3,000 .
Example
- Profit Target = $3,000
- Balance required for passing = $53,000

Max Drawdown
EOD Trailing
$2,000
The Maximum Drawdown rule establishes an end-of-day trailing limit (the Maximum Drawdown Limit ) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions ) cannot hit at any point. If equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated and the Evaluation is unsuccessful.
The Maximum Drawdown Limit is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Simulated Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount .
The limit can only increase , never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital , it locks permanently at that figure for the remaining duration of the Evaluation, unless (i) you pay a Reset Fee, or (ii) you breach the Maximum Drawdown Limit and wait for the next monthly subscription.
The Maximum Drawdown Amount is $2,000 .
Example
Limit for Day 1:
- Initial Simulated Capital = $50,000
- Calculation = $50,000 – $2,000 = $48,000
- Limit for Day 1 = $48,000
Limit for Day 2:
- Closing Balance of Day 1 = $51,000
- Calculation = $51,000 – $2,000 = $49,000
- Limit for Day 2 = $49,000
Limit for Day 3:
- Closing Balance of Day 2 = $50,500
- Calculation = $51,000 – $2,000 = $49,000 (Because the Closing Balance of Day 1 was higher than that of Day 2, the highest preceding balance of $51,000 is used for the calculation of the limits.)
- Limit for Day 3 = $49,000
Limit for Day 4:
- Closing Balance of Day 3 = $52,500
- Calculation = $52,500 – $2,000 = $50,500 (The calculated limit exceeds the Initial Simulated Capital, so the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at the Initial Simulated Capital instead.)
- Limit for Day 4 = $50,000 (From now on, the limit is equal to the Initial Simulated Capital.)

Daily Loss Limit
$1,000
The Daily Loss Limit rule establishes a limit (the Daily Loss Limit ) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions ) cannot hit in a single trading day. If the equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated.
The Daily Loss Limit is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the account balance at the start of that trading day and the applicable Daily Loss Limit Amount .
The Daily Loss Limit Amount is $1,000 .
On the first day, the account balance used for the limit calculation is the Initial Simulated Capital.
The Pro Account applies a Hard Daily Loss Limit in the Evaluation. If the limit is breached, the Evaluation is unsuccessful.

Consistency Rule
40%
To pass the Evaluation, the Consistency Rule requires that your single most profitable trading day (the Best Day ) does not represent more than 40% of your total profit .
The total profit is the cumulative sum of closed profits and losses across all trading days in the Evaluation phase. The Best Day is defined as the single day on which the highest closed profit was achieved, measured at the end of the trading day.
Exceeding the Consistency Rule limit is not treated as a rule breach. However, you must continue trading to accumulate additional profit until your Best Day represents 40% or less of your total profit in order to pass the Evaluation.
Example
P&L per Day:
- Day 1 = closed profit = $2,000
- Day 2 = closed profit = $1,000
Results at the end of Day 2:
- Total Profit = $3,000
- The Best Day is Day 1. On this day, 66.7% of the total profit was achieved ( $2,000 of $3,000 = 66.7%).
Because the profit achieved on the Best Day exceeds the 40% allowed limit, the Consistency Rule is not satisfied. If Day 1 remains your Best Day, your total profit will need to reach at least $5,000 to pass.

Trading Objectives
Max Drawdown
EOD Trailing
$2,000
The Maximum Drawdown rule establishes an end-of-day trailing limit (the Maximum Drawdown Limit ) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions ) cannot hit at any point. If equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated and the account is terminated.
The Maximum Drawdown Limit is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Simulated Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount .
The limit can only increase , never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital , it locks permanently at that figure for the remaining life of the account.
The Maximum Drawdown Amount is $2,000 .
Example
Limit for Day 1:
- Initial Simulated Capital = $50,000
- Calculation = $50,000 – $2,000 = $48,000
- Limit for Day 1 = $48,000
Limit for Day 2:
- Closing Balance of Day 1 = $51,500
- Calculation = $51,500 – $2,000 = $49,500
- Limit for Day 2 = $49,500
Limit for Day 3:
- Closing Balance of Day 2 = $51,000
- Calculation = $51,500 – $2,000 = $49,500 (Because the Closing Balance of Day 1 was higher than that of Day 2, the highest preceding balance of $51,500 is used for the calculation of the limits.)
- Limit for Day 3 = $49,500
Limit for Day 4:
- Closing Balance of Day 3 = $53,000
- Calculation = $53,000 – $2,000 = $51,000 (The calculated limit exceeds the Initial Simulated Capital, so the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at the Initial Simulated Capital instead.)
- Limit for Day 4 = $50,000 (From now on, the limit is equal to the Initial Simulated Capital.)

The Max Contracts rule limits the maximum number of contracts you may hold simultaneously at any given time, across all instruments.
The Max Contracts limit is 2 contracts at the start of your Sim-Funded Account and can increase through the Scaling Plan.
The Scaling Plan allows you to increase your Max Contracts limit as your trading performance grows. As you demonstrate consistent profitability on the Sim-Funded Account, additional contract capacity becomes available without the need to purchase a new account.
The Scaling Plan operates on a milestone-based system: each time your end-of-day (EOD) Profit above the Initial Simulated Capital reaches a defined threshold, your Max Contracts limit adjusts by a set amount.
The following list describes the range of the Profit above the Initial Simulated Capital, together with the number of contracts allowed to be opened:
- $0–$999 → 2 minis or standard / 20 micros contracts
- $1,000–$1,999 → 3 minis or standard / 30 micros contracts
- $2,000–$2,999 → 5 minis or standard / 50 micros contracts
- $3,000–$4,499 → 5 minis or standard / 50 micros contracts
- $4,500+ → 5 minis or standard / 50 micros contracts
Example
You open your Sim-Funded Account with $50,000 and a limit of 2 contracts . After a run of profitable trading days, your account balance closes at $53,000 . Starting from the next trading day, your Max Contracts limit automatically increases to 5 contracts .
If the following day the end-of-day balance decreases back to $50,000 , the number of contracts will be adjusted the next trading day back to 2 contracts .

Payouts
Payout Eligibility
4 days @ $150+
To become eligible to request a payout, you must trade on a minimum number of trading days within the payout cycle, each meeting a minimum daily profit threshold.
- Minimum number of Qualifying Days: 4 trading days
- Minimum daily P&L required: $150 per Qualifying Day
Qualifying Days are reviewed at the end of the trading day.
Example
Trader achieves the following end-of-day P&L on 4 different trading days:
- Day 1: $180
- Day 2: $220
- Day 3: $120
- Day 4: $190
Result: The end-of-day P&L of Day 3 did not reach the $150+ requirement in the closed P&L, which is required to consider the trading day a Qualifying Day.
Since the other 3 days met the requirement, 3 out of 4 Qualifying Days are currently met. You are not yet eligible to request a payout and must continue trading until all 4 days within a payout cycle meet the threshold.

Trading Objectives
Max Drawdown
EOD Trailing
$2,000
The Maximum Drawdown rule establishes an end-of-day trailing limit (the Maximum Drawdown Limit ) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions ) cannot hit at any point. If equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated and the account is terminated.
The Maximum Drawdown Limit is calculated daily as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount .
The limit can only increase , never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Capital , it locks permanently at that figure for the remaining life of the account.
The Maximum Drawdown Amount is $2,000 .
Example
Limit for Day 1:
- Initial Capital = $2,000
- Calculation = $2,000 – $2,000 = $0
- Limit for Day 1 = $0
Limit for Day 2:
- Closing Balance of Day 1 = $3,000
- Calculation = $3,000 – $2,000 = $1,000
- Limit for Day 2 = $1,000
Limit for Day 3:
- Closing Balance of Day 2 = $2,500
- Calculation = $3,000 – $2,000 = $1,000 (Because the Closing Balance of Day 1 was higher than that of Day 2, the highest preceding balance of $3,000 is used for the calculation of the limits.)
- Limit for Day 3 = $1,000
Limit for Day 4:
- Closing Balance of Day 3 = $4,500
- Calculation = $4,500 – $2,000 = $2,500 (The calculated limit exceeds the Initial Capital, so the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at the Initial Capital instead.)
- Limit for Day 4 = $2,000 (From now on, the limit is equal to the Initial Capital.)

Max Concurrent Accounts
3 accounts
Can I hold other accounts alongside a Live Funded Account?
Once you hold a Live Funded Account, you cannot hold Evaluation or Sim-Funded Accounts. You may hold a maximum of 3 concurrent Live Funded Accounts at any one time, across all account sizes and product types.
When you move to a Live Funded Account:
- Sim-Funded Accounts with at least one approved payout are eligible to move to Live Funded.
- Sim-Funded Accounts without a payout and any ongoing Evaluations are closed.
For each closed account, we refund the last payment made for it. For a Sim-Funded Account, this is the last payment you made for the Evaluation you passed. Refunds cover the amount you actually paid, after any discounts. Reset fees are not refunded.

Payout cap
Safety Net
$2,000
Your Live Funded Account starts with an Initial Capital equal to the Maximum Drawdown Amount . The Safety Net is the same amount.
The Safety Net is $2,000 .
Because the Maximum Drawdown Limit is an end-of-day trailing limit, it rises with your closing balance until it locks at the Initial Capital . This happens once your balance has grown by the amount of the Safety Net .
To become eligible to request a payout, you must clear the Safety Net. Payouts are available from the balance above the Initial Capital.
Example
- Initial Capital = $2,000
- Safety Net = $2,000
- Balance after building the Safety Net = $4,000 (the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at $2,000 )
- Balance later = $5,500
- Withdrawable amount = $3,500 (everything above $2,000 )
If equity hits $2,000 , the account is terminated due to a breach of the Maximum Drawdown.
