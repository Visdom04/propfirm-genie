EOD Daily Loss Limit by size and what happens when you hit it.
Updated over a week ago
Copy for LLM
EOD accounts have a Daily Loss Limit (DLL) : the most you can lose in one trading day. It's separate from the max drawdown.
Size
Daily Loss Limit
25K
$800
50K
$1,250
150K
$2,000
300K
$3,250
If you hit it
- Open positions are closed and the account is Paused until the next trading day starts (6:00 PM ET). It isn't failed.
- The DLL is measured from your balance at the start of the day. It doesn't trail intraday.
