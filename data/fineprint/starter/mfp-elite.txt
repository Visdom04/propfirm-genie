Elite Accounts
Copy page Copy page
Elite plan sizes, prices, and daily loss limit options, evaluation and Sim Funded rules, leverage and exposure caps, reduced fees, funded allocation limits, and payouts.
Copy page Copy page
Elite is a 1-Step evaluation with a static maximum loss limit, no consistency
rule, and boosted leverage on NAS100, SP500, US30, gold, and oil. It comes in $5K, $10K,
$25K, and $50K account sizes, and every size has two options: 3% DLL ,
which adds a 3% daily loss limit at a lower price, or No DLL , which has no
daily loss limit. Passing leads to a Sim Funded Elite account with the same
rules. Open the
challenge selector and choose the
Elite tab to buy.
​

any rolling 7-day period; see No DLL purchase limit .

own limit; see
Funded allocation .
​
No DLL purchase limit
Elite No DLL evaluations have a weekly purchase limit of $250,000 in total
account size per rolling 7-day period. The limit covers all Elite No DLL
account sizes combined, so you could buy ten $ 25K accounts, five $50K accounts,
or any mix totaling $ 250,000 or less within any 7 days. An order that brings
the total to exactly $250,000 is allowed.
- The limit counts account size, not price or number of accounts.
- Each purchase counts for 7 days (168 hours) from the time you bought it. You
can buy again as earlier purchases roll out of the window; there is no fixed
weekly reset.
- Failed, reset, or deleted accounts still count until 7 days after their
purchase.
- Refunded purchases still count until 7 days after their purchase. A refund
does not return any of the limit.
- Failed or abandoned payments do not count. If a payment marked failed later
goes through, that purchase counts for 7 days from when it completed.
- Elite 3% DLL accounts are unaffected and stay under their usual limits.
If an order would go over the limit, checkout does not take payment. It shows
how much you bought in the last 7 days, how much remains, a countdown, and the
exact date and time in your timezone when the selected order fits. It also
offers a Try Elite 3% DLL button that switches checkout to the same size
with the 3% daily loss limit. Most traders buy well under this amount in a
typical week and will not notice any difference.
​
DLL options
Elite pricing cards start with 3% DLL enabled and keep that option until
you change it. Turn off the daily loss limit toggle on a card to choose
No DLL for that size. You can also choose the option at checkout. The two options
are identical except for the daily loss limit:
Option Daily loss limit Everything else
3% DLL 3% of starting balance, measured from the day’s starting equity Same Elite rules on this page
No DLL None Same Elite rules on this page
A 3% DLL account follows the same 3% rule as Select, Prime, and Signature:
- The floor is the day’s starting equity, including open-position profit or
loss, minus 3% of the starting balance. On an Elite $50K account the
allowance is $1,500.
- Equity that reaches the floor breaches the account, fails it, and closes its
positions.
- The day boundary matches the other plans; see
Challenges and Rules .
- The rule applies in both the evaluation and Sim Funded stages, together with
the static maximum loss limit.
The option is part of the account you buy. You cannot add or remove the daily
loss limit after purchase.
​
Elite prize accounts
Elite accounts can also be won in giveaways. A won Elite account follows the
same Elite rules as a bought No DLL account of its size, with no daily loss
limit. Giveaways can also award a
$1K Elite account, which cannot be bought. On a $1K Elite account the profit
target is $100, the maximum loss limit is $50, and the minimum payout
request is $30 before the split ($24 to you on 80/20).

The profit target and the maximum loss limit (MLL) use account equity,
including open-position profit or loss. There is no minimum trading-day count
or evaluation time limit.
The plan adds no evaluation rules beyond this table. Platform-wide rules still
apply, including the trading policy , prohibited trading
activity, account inactivity, market exposure caps, and the platform terms.
​
How static drawdown works
The loss threshold is the starting balance minus the MLL, and it is fixed for
the life of the account. It does not trail profits, intraday highs, or
end-of-day highs.
For example, an Elite $25K account has a $1,250 MLL, so its loss threshold is
$23,750. If equity grows to $27,000, the threshold stays at $23,750, leaving
$3,250 of room. Equity that reaches $23,750 breaches the account, fails it,
and closes its positions.

Leverage and exposure
Elite accounts use boosted leverage on NAS100, SP500, US30, gold, and oil. Each cap
is a multiple of the account’s starting balance and applies to each account
separately, in both the evaluation and Sim Funded stages:
Markets Maximum leverage Elite $5K exposure Elite $10K exposure Elite $25K exposure Elite $50K exposure
NAS100, SP500 40x $200,000 $400,000 $1,000,000 $2,000,000
US30 25x $125,000 $250,000 $625,000 $1,250,000
Gold (XAU) 20x $100,000 $200,000 $500,000 $1,000,000
Oil (CL, BRENTOIL) 10x $50,000 $100,000 $250,000 $500,000
- Exposure is the full value of your open positions in that group, long plus
short. NAS100 and SP500 share the indices cap, US30 has its own cap, and CL
and BRENTOIL share the oil cap. Positions still open on the retiring GOLD
market count toward the gold cap together with XAU.
- The account’s total exposure across every market is capped at 40x its
starting balance.
- Every other market keeps its normal maximum leverage, shown on the
Market Exposure Caps page.
Choose Elite and your account size there to see the current group caps,
account exposure limit, and market caps for that size.
- Market exposure caps still apply on top of
the Elite caps. Each Elite account has its own market exposure cap: your
other Elite accounts of the same size do not share or use up its capacity.
The order ticket shows the limit for the selected market as Account
Exposure Cap , and the leverage selector goes up to the Elite maximum for that
market. An order that would exceed the leverage or an exposure cap is
rejected. Reducing or closing a position is always allowed.

Fees
Elite accounts pay reduced fees on four markets, in both the evaluation and
Sim Funded stages:
Markets Elite commission per fill Elite daily swap rate Standard commission Standard daily swap rate
NAS100, SP500 0.001% 0.01% 0.005% 0.015%
Gold, WTI Oil 0.001% 0.015% 0.005% 0.015%
- On a $10,000 fill in NAS100, SP500, Gold, or WTI Oil, the Elite commission
is $0.10 instead of $0.50.
- A NAS100 or SP500 position worth $10,000 pays $1.00 in swap fees over 24
hourly charges, instead of $1.50.
- Every other market, including other indices, other commodities such as
silver and Brent, crypto, forex, and stocks, uses the standard rates.
Read Commissions and Fees for how commissions
and hourly swap fees are calculated.
​
Accounts per order
One order can include up to 10 accounts at $5K or $10K, up to 4 at $25K, or
up to 2 at $50K, all of the same size and option. You can place as many
orders as you like.
​
Sim Funded
Passing the evaluation leads to a Sim Funded Elite account. It keeps the same
rules as the evaluation, without the profit target:
- the same starting balance;
- the same 5% static MLL and loss threshold, which does not move after a
withdrawal;
- the same leverage and exposure caps;
- the same daily loss limit option: a 3% daily loss limit on a 3% DLL
account, or none on a No DLL account;
- no consistency rule.
Sim Funded accounts use simulated capital and simulated execution, like every
funded account.
​
High-impact news restriction
The high-impact news restriction applies to Sim Funded Elite accounts. During
the evaluation, news trading is not restricted.
Time from the release What you can do
More than 10 minutes before Trade normally. A position already open can be held through the release.
10 minutes to 2 minutes before No new exposure in the affected market: no new positions, no adding, and no reversing.
2 minutes before to 2 minutes after No manual trades of any kind. Close or reduce a position before this window if you want to be flat.
Restricted events are every event classified as high impact (red folder) on
the economic calendar, including FOMC decisions and statements, CPI, PPI,
Non-Farm Payrolls, GDP, Retail Sales, employment reports, PCE inflation, and
ECB, BOE, and BOJ rate decisions. For individual stocks and equity perpetuals,
the company’s earnings announcements are restricted too. Read the full policy
in the FAQ under
Is news trading allowed on funded? .
Profit from trades that break the restriction can be excluded during payout
review.
​
No Live stage
Elite has no Live stage and no promotion to live trading. Elite accounts
remain Sim Funded.
​
Funded allocation
Each customer can hold up to $100,000 of active Sim Funded Elite
allocation , summed by the original starting size of each funded Elite
account across every size and both options. That allows 20 × $5K,
10 × $10K, 4 × $25K, 2 × $50K, or any mix that totals $100,000 or less. Profits, losses, and withdrawals do not
change the allocation an account uses. A funded account that fails or is
closed stops using allocation.
Elite is its own model type, so Elite allocation also counts toward the
$300K maximum funded allocation
across all models.
Active Elite evaluations are limited separately from funded Elite allocation,
so you can pass more Elite evaluations than can be funded at once. When a
passed evaluation would take
your Elite funded allocation above $100,000, or your total above $300,000,
it keeps its Passed status and enters Funding Review after identity
verification and agreement signing, instead of being funded automatically.
Review does not guarantee funding, and any approval must still fit within both
limits. If a funded account fails or closes while the evaluation waits and it
then fits within both limits, it is funded automatically. See Becoming Funded .

When you can request On demand On demand On demand On demand

These payout terms are the same for the 3% DLL and No DLL options. The split is
80% to you and 20% to MyFundedPerps, or 90% to you with the 90/10 upgrade.
- On demand: you can request from your first day funded. Elite has no
waiting period, minimum trading days, consistency requirement, or profit
target before a request, and no daily request limit: you can request again
as soon as your previous request finishes. The account must be flat with no
pending request. See Payouts .
- Requesting is not payment: every request is reviewed, and trading on the
account is locked while it is pending. Review and delivery take time, so a
request does not mean an immediate transfer.
- All profit: you can request all of the profit in the current payout
cycle. Profit you leave in the account when a payout is approved becomes a
balance buffer and cannot be withdrawn later.
- Minimum: the minimum is measured before the split, as the gross amount
deducted from the account. On $25K, a $250 gross minimum pays you $200.
- 90/10 upgrade: choose it at checkout to receive 90% of each payout
instead of 80%. It is part of the account you buy and cannot be added to an
existing account.
Payouts remain subject to plan terms, eligibility, and review.
​
Withdrawals and your loss cushion
A payout deducts its full gross amount from the balance, which reduces the room
above the loss threshold by the same amount. The threshold itself does not
move.
For example, an Elite $25K account at $27,000 equity with no open positions
has $2,000 of profit and $3,250 of room above its $23,750 threshold.
Requesting all of it deducts $2,000 gross and pays you $1,600 ($1,800 with
the 90/10 upgrade). The balance
returns to $25,000, the threshold stays at $23,750, and the room above it
returns to the original $1,250.
Select, Prime, and Signature work differently: their loss threshold locks to
the starting balance on the first withdrawal. Elite never has that lock.
On a 3% DLL account, a withdrawal also never lowers the daily loss floor below
where it started that day. See
Daily loss limit after a withdrawal .
​
Lifetime payout cap
Elite payouts count toward the
$100,000 lifetime payout cap
per user, combined across every plan and counted after the profit split. This
is separate from the $100,000 Elite funded allocation cap: the payout cap
limits the money you can receive, while the funded cap limits the total
starting size of your active Sim Funded Elite accounts.
