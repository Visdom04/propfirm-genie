Futures Rewards & Payout Structure
At Blue Guardian Futures, our payout system is designed to balance flexibility, consistency, and long-term account growth. Each funded model has its own payout structure, caps, and requirements tailored to different trading styles.
How do payouts affect my drawdown?
After any approved payout or withdrawal, your drawdown floor will automatically lock at:
Starting Balance + $100
This ensures your account remains active and prevents withdrawals down to the exact breach level.

Minimum Withdrawal
Express: $250 via Crypto or Rise. Standard, Reserve and Direct: $500 via Crypto or Rise.
Maximum Crypto Payout
$1,000 per payout request (all account types). Payouts above $1,000 must be requested via Rise, which has no maximum payout limit.
Payout Processing Fee
All payouts are subject to a 3% processing fee to cover payment processing and transaction costs.
This means the final amount received may differ slightly from the amount displayed on your payout certificate.
