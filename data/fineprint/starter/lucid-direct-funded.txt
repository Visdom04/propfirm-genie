LucidDirect Funded Account Overview
The LucidDirect is a simulated straight-to-funded account which does not have an evaluation phase. When you purchase a LucidDirect account, you start building simulated capital to earn payouts immediately and work towards being moved live. See account sizes and details below.

LucidDirect Funded Account Benefits
- Skip the evaluation phase and go straight-to-funded
- Objectives on our dashboard update in real-time within 5-30 minutes of your last closed trade
- Scaling DLL that grows with your account balance provides flexible risk
- No scaling plan, access to max contract size immediately
- No simulated payout caps
