A "Lightning Funded" account allows traders to skip the typical evaluation phases and begin trading simulated funds immediately. This gives Tradeify traders direct access to a simulated funded account with the potential to start earning profits towards their payout from day one.
**⚠️ Important: Accounts purchased before the new dashboard launch retain their original parameters, including on reset.**
Tradeify offers Lightning Funded account sizes of $25k, $50k, $100k & $150k. The rules for the Lightning Funded are stated in the table below:

Lightning Funded Accounts use an End of Day Trailing Max Drawdown, which is calculated and based on the account balance at the end of the trading day.
For more information on this type of drawdown, see our full article [here](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495897-rules-trailing-max-drawdowns).
This offers more flexibility during the trading day, because intraday fluctuations do not move the drawdown level - it only updates at the end of the day. The level itself is still enforced in real time: if your net liquidation value touches it at any moment during the day, the account fails immediately, even if you recover before the close.
For these accounts, we've implemented a **progressive [Consistency Rule](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468320-rules-consistency-rule)** that starts at **20%** for your first payout and gradually increases to ensure sustained performance. This graduated approach helps us identify traders committed to long-term success and continuous improvement, rather than those relying on short-term, high-risk strategies. As you advance through multiple payouts, the increasing consistency requirements demonstrate your ability to maintain steady and reliable performance over time.
By maintaining consistent trading habits, traders can better manage risk and achieve sustainable growth—aligning with our goal of fostering a stable and profitable trading environment
## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What is the maximum position size for my 50K Lightning account?**
A: For accounts purchased after September 12, 2025: **4 mini contracts OR 40 micro contracts**. Legacy accounts purchased before that date have 5 minis / 50 micros.
**Q: Can I hold both mini and micro contracts at the same time?**
A: Yes. Minis and micros may now be held at the same time, as long as your combined position stays within your contract limit (10 micros = 1 mini). Holding them in opposing directions on the same or a correlated product is still a hedging violation. See [Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-trading-micros-or-minis) for details.
