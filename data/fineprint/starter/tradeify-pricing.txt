**Tradeify Pricing Reference** — This article is a comprehensive pricing guide for Tradeify Futures Prop trading accounts. All plans are **one-time purchases** with no recurring billing.
## Account Types Overview
Tradeify offers three account types, all available in 25K, 50K, 100K, and 150K sizes:
## Growth Evaluation Pricing
Growth Evaluations are one-time purchases. Pass the evaluation to get funded. Resets are available if your account fails.
Growth 25K costs $99, and a reset costs $60. Growth 50K costs $145, and a reset costs $95. Growth 100K costs $255, and a reset costs $169. Growth 150K costs $369, and a reset costs $229.

## Select Evaluation Pricing
Select Evaluations are one-time purchases. Pass the evaluation and choose between Select Daily or Select Flex payout paths. Resets are available if your account fails.
#### **Select Evaluation with 40% Consistency:**
With the standard 40% consistency rule, Select 25K costs $109, and a reset costs $75. Select 50K costs $165, and a reset costs $109. Select 100K costs $265, and a reset costs $169. Select 150K costs $369, and a reset costs $239. Select 300K (limited release) costs $349 (V2; V1 was $449), and resets are not available.

**Select Evaluation with 50% Consistency Add-on:**

The 50% Consistency Add-on raises your consistency limit from 40% to 50%, allowing a single day to account for up to half of your total profit — meaning you can pass your evaluation in as little as 2 trading days instead of 3.
With the 50% Consistency Add-on, Select 25K costs $135, and a reset costs $90. Select 50K costs $205, and a reset costs $135. Select 100K costs $329, and a reset costs $209. Select 150K costs $459, and a reset costs $299. The 50% Consistency Add-on is not available on the Select 300K.

## Lightning Funded Pricing
Lightning Funded accounts are one-time purchases with no evaluation required. You are funded instantly. No resets are available — if the account fails, a new one must be purchased.
Lightning 25K costs $345. Lightning 50K costs $492. Lightning 100K costs $660. Lightning 150K costs $796.
## Bundle Discounts
Tradeify offers a 5-account bundle discount on select Evaluations. At checkout, tick the **Bundle and save** option to buy 5 qualifying accounts of the same type and size in one order and receive an automatic 5% discount. This is the only way to buy more than one account in a single checkout — there is no option to buy 2, 3, or 4 together.
### Eligible Bundle Accounts
All 5 accounts in a bundle must be the same account type and size. 100K and 150K sizes and Lightning Funded accounts are not currently eligible for bundle pricing.

## Activation Fees
There are **no activation fees** for any current Tradeify account type. The purchase price covers everything needed to start trading.

## Trading Commission Fees
All trades incur commission fees. These are per-contract, round-trip (open + close) fees charged by the exchange and broker. Common instruments:
The round-trip commission per contract is $5.76 for ES (E-mini S&P 500), $5.76 for NQ (E-mini Nasdaq), $1.82 for MES (Micro E-mini S&P), $1.82 for MNQ (Micro E-mini Nasdaq), $5.98 for CL (Crude Oil), and $6.20 for GC (Gold).

## Refund Policy
All sales are final. Tradeify does not offer refunds on any purchases. This includes account purchases, resets, and any other fees. Please review all account details before purchasing. **One exception:** if a promo or discount code was not applied to your purchase, that is handled separately — contact support with your transaction details and we will get it corrected.
## Frequently Asked Questions
### Are Tradeify accounts subscription-based or one-time purchases?
All Tradeify accounts are one-time purchases. There is no recurring billing or monthly subscription. You pay once and trade until you pass (evaluations) or for the life of the account (Lightning Funded).
### What is the cheapest Tradeify account?
The Growth 25K Evaluation is the most affordable option. Check the pricing tables above for current prices. Promo codes may offer additional discounts — check the Tradeify website for any active promotions.
### What is the difference between Growth, Select, and Lightning?
**Growth:** Evaluation required. Has Daily Loss Limit. No consistency rule during the evaluation (35% on funded payouts). Most affordable option. **Select:** Evaluation required. No DLL during evaluation. 40% consistency during evaluation only. Choose Daily or Flex payout paths after passing. **Lightning:** No evaluation — funded instantly. Progressive consistency (20% → 25% → 30%). No resets available. Higher price point.
### Can I get a refund if my account fails?
No. All sales are final per our Terms of Service. If a Growth or Select evaluation fails, you can purchase a reset at a reduced price to try again. Lightning Funded accounts do not have resets — a new account must be purchased.
### Are there any hidden fees?
No hidden fees. The only costs are: (1) the account purchase price, (2) trading commissions on each trade, and (3) optional reset fees if your evaluation fails. Market data is free as long as you sign the Non-Professional Agreement. All brokers (Tradovate, Rithmic, WealthCharts) are the same price — no platform surcharges.
### Do you offer promo codes or discounts?
Tradeify periodically offers promotional discounts. Check the Tradeify website or dashboard for any currently active promo codes. Promo codes are entered at checkout. Tradeify also offers a permanent 5-account bundle discount on Growth 25K, Growth 50K, Select 25K, and Select 50K Evaluations — see the Bundle Discounts section above. If you completed a purchase and a promo code was not applied — you forgot to enter it, or you entered it and were still charged the full amount — contact support with your transaction details. This is handled separately from the refund policy.
### Can I buy multiple accounts at a discount?
Yes. Tradeify offers a 5-account bundle discount on Growth 25K, Growth 50K, Select 25K, and Select 50K Evaluations. At checkout, tick the Bundle and save option to buy 5 of the same eligible account in one order and the 5% bundle discount applies automatically. It also stacks with any active promo code. The discount only applies in a single transaction and won't apply retroactively to a separate, later order. See the Bundle Discounts section above for full details.
### Can I buy 2, 3, or 4 accounts in one order?
No. The only multi-account checkout is the 5-account Bundle and save option. To buy any other number, purchase accounts individually (one per checkout) at full price. Growth evaluations have no purchase limit; Select evaluations are limited to 15 per 30-day period (300K purchases do not count). The 5-account cap applies to active Sim Funded accounts per household.
### What is the profit split?
Sim Funded accounts (Growth, Select, Lightning) use a 90/10 profit split — you keep 90% of your profits, Tradeify receives 10%. Elite Live accounts use an 80/20 split. This applies from your very first payout.
### What platforms can I trade on?
At checkout, you select one of three brokers: Tradovate, Rithmic, or WealthCharts. Each broker gives access to different trading platforms. Tradovate gives access to NinjaTrader and TradingView. Rithmic gives access to Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, and R|Trader. WealthCharts is its own broker and platform — it can only be used to access the WealthCharts platform. See the Supported Platforms article for details.
