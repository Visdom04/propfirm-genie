What are the available Account sizes and their Prices in FundedNext Futures?
Updated in the last hour
Table of contents
TL;DR: FundedNext Futures offers multiple account sizes across different challenge models, including $25K, $50K, $100K, and $150K accounts. Pricing varies depending on the selected challenge type — Rapid Pro and Daily, Legacy, or Flex —with each model offering different trading conditions, rules, and features. The Flex Account also includes a 95% Reward Share for traders seeking higher profit potential.
1. Direct Account
The FundedNext Futures Direct Account is designed for traders who want to skip the evaluation entirely and start on a FundedNext Account from day one, with no profit goal to withdraw, a competitive 90% Reward Share, and a 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule that rewards steady trading. Available as a $50K account, it offers the fastest and most affordable path to trading a FundedNext Account.

The FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge is designed for traders who want speed and flexibility in a single challenge, with a fast one-day pass, an End-of-Day trailing drawdown, and two selectable paths — Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily. The Rapid Challenge is available in multiple account sizes, allowing traders to choose the path and model that best suit their trading style and risk management preferences.

- How does Flex offer work?
Here's how Flex pricing works. The first two purchases made with the coupon get the best pricing: $74.99 at 50K, $144.99 at 100K, and $259.99 at 150K. From the third purchase onward, the same coupon applies a different discount, bringing the price to $84.99, $159.99, and $289.99.
