Payout Rules
Daily Payout Requests
You can request a payout any day. We answer within up to two business days and start the payment within up to two business days of approval. Wire transfers go out weekly. Futures funded accounts are eligible once the account is above +1% profit. No per-payout cap. When your total payouts reach $100,000, your next request pauses for a review of up to 5 business days.

02 Why Payout Rules Matter
Payout rules are one of the clearest ways to compare prop firms. Traders Launch keeps payout eligibility, the $100,000 payout review and processing terms visible, with requests subject to program rules and Terms.
