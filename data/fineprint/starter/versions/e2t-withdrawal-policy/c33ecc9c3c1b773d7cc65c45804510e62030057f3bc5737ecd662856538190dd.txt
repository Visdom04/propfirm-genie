Minimum Number of Profitable Trading Days
Traders must complete at least 5 profitable trading days before each withdrawal request . These days do not need to be consecutive.
To qualify as a profitable trading day, the account must generate a minimum daily profit based on its account size and program, as outlined below.

Additional Conditions
- Before every withdrawal: Traders must complete 5 new qualifying profitable trading days before submitting each withdrawal request. Profitable trading days counted toward a previous withdrawal cannot be reused.
- Non-consecutive days: The 5 profitable trading days do not need to occur consecutively.
- Account balance: A profitable trading day counts toward the requirement even if the account balance remains below its original starting balance, provided the minimum daily profit threshold is met.
Example
A trader with a Trader Career Path® $50,000 (TCP50) account starts the trading day with a balance of $49,000 and closes the day at $49,150. Although the account balance remains below the original $50,000 starting balance, the trader has generated a daily profit of $150. This counts as 1 of the 5 required profitable trading days toward the next withdrawal request.

*LiveSim Max Withdrawal is the total you can withdraw from a LiveSim account before you are automatically moved to a Live account. It is not a limit on any single withdrawal.
Withdrawals are processed weekly on Wednesdays via Rise or other platforms. Requests must be emailed to the proprietary firm by 2:00 PM CT on the preceding Friday. The minimum net withdrawal amount is $100. Due to regulatory compliance, Rise does not support payouts for traders in certain U.S. territories (Iowa, Minnesota, South Carolina, Guam, Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands) or Haiti.
Withdrawal methods are not optional, and the prop firm retains the right to onboard customers to their payment provider of choice, with some options available only in select regions. Direct crypto withdrawals are only available in select cases.
