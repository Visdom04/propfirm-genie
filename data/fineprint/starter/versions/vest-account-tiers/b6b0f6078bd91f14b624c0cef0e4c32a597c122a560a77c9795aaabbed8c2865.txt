## Account Tiers
Full pricing and configuration reference for both Vest Capital paths.
* * *
### With Evaluation[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/account-tiers#with-evaluation)
1-Step evaluation accounts are priced from four independent choices. The fixed 2-Step configuration is described separately below:
* **Tier** — Silver ($5,000), Gold ($10,000), or Platinum ($25,000)
* **Profit Target** — 10% or 20% of capital
* **Daily Loss Limit** — 3%, 4%, or none
* **Profit split** — 80/20 or 90/10 (you/Vest)
**1-Step**
Priced from four independent choices:
* **Tier** — Silver ($5,000), Gold ($10,000), or Platinum ($25,000)
* **Profit Target** — 10% or 20% of capital
* **Daily Loss Limit** — 3%, 4%, or none
* **Profit split** — 80/20 or 90/10 (you/Vest)
There are 12 combinations of profit target, daily loss limit and profit split across 3 capital sizes: 36 available 1-Step configurations. Select 1-Step and configure these options in the purchase flow.
Example configurations
Examples of available 1-Step settings:
**1-Step**
10% Target, 3% Daily Loss Limit, 80/20 split
**1-Step, Higher Profit Target**
20% Target, 3% Daily Loss Limit, 80/20 split
**1-Step, No Daily Loss Limit**
10% Target, no Daily Loss Limit, 80/20 split
All combinations in the pricing matrix are available in the 1-Step configuration controls.

#### **2-Step**[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/account-tiers#id-2-step)
One fixed configuration per tier. Priced lower than the equivalent 1-Step account, in exchange for a two-stage Profit Goal.

Fixed on every 2-Step account:
* **Profit Goal** — two stages: 10% of capital, then a further 5%
* **Daily Loss Limit** — 3%
* **Profit split** — 80/20 (you/Vest)
* **Max. Drawdown** — 6% (see below)
Select 2-Step in the evaluation purchase flow to see its fixed settings and pricing.
#### Max. Drawdown[](http://docs.vestmarkets.com/vest-capital/account-tiers#max.-drawdown)
Fixed at **6% of capital**, the same across every combination on a given tier. None of the four purchase choices affect it.
