EOD consistency: 50% in the evaluation, 30% per Pro payout cycle, with an example.
Updated over a week ago
Copy for LLM
EOD accounts have two consistency rules: 50% in the evaluation and 30% per payout cycle in the Pro Account.
How it's calculated
Largest single-day profit ÷ total profit = consistency %

Example (Pro): $3,000 made this cycle, best day $1,200. 1,200 ÷ 3,000 = 40%, over 30%. You'd need $4,000 in the cycle before requesting a payout.
If you're over the limit
Consistency is checked at the end of every trading day. A day that ends over the limit isn't a Qualifying Day, and you can't pass or request a payout until you're back within it. Keep trading to bring it down, and check your numbers with the Consistency Calculator .
